## NN-001: Rain-Soaked Neon Alley
**Category:** Night & Neon | **Subcategory:** Neon Urban
**Best Platform:** NanoBanana

> A narrow Tokyo back-alley after heavy rain, every surface transformed into a mirror reflecting the dense vertical signage in hot pink, electric blue, and amber neon. Photographed with a 35mm lens at f/1.4 from standing height looking down the alley's length, the wet asphalt creates perfect inverted reflections that double the visual density of light. Steam rises from a ramen shop vent, diffusing the nearest pink sign into a soft glow zone. A single figure with a clear umbrella walks away mid-frame, their silhouette dark against the neon wash. Puddles in uneven pavement show individual ripples from continuing drizzle that distort the reflected colors. The color temperature ranges from 2700K warm tungsten shop interiors to 6500K cool blue signage within a single frame. Electrical cables overhead create dark geometric lines against the illuminated mist. Natural sensor noise is present in shadow areas.

**Key Techniques:** Wet surface neon mirror reflections, extreme mixed color temperature coexistence, steam diffusion of point light sources
