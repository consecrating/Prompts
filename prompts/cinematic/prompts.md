# Cinematic Prompts (25)

## CN-001: Shadow of Intent
> Rain-slicked city street at night through anamorphic lens — oval bokeh, horizontal flares from passing headlights. 45-year-old man in dark wool overcoat stands beneath single streetlamp, face half-illuminated in hard sodium pool, other half pure black. Venetian blind shadows stripe wet sidewalk. Rain creates visible backlit streaks. Extreme noir ratio 8:1. Puddles reflect as distorted mirrors. Cooke Anamorphic 50mm T2, Steadicam chest height. Storaro-inspired tonal control.

## CN-002: Colony Wake
> Deep-space cryo bay interior. 32-year-old woman emerging from frosted glass pod, slow dolly-in from wide to MCU. Practical LED strips in cool blue-white line pod interior, amber emergency indicators at periphery. Cryogenic emergence physiology: pallor, frost on eyebrows, blue lips, shoulder goosebumps. Condensation fog rolls from pod catching strip lighting. Pod shows hose connections, monitoring readouts, dendritic frost crystals. Damp flat-pressed hair. 32mm Panavision T1.4. Lubezki single-source motivation.

## CN-003: The Correspondence
> Candlelit 1840s English study. 55-year-old woman in dark green silk taffeta bends over writing desk with steel-nib pen. Lit entirely by three candles and dying fire — warm pools with rapid inverse-square falloff. Candlelight flicker frozen at high shutter. Quill casts tiny paper shadow. Period inkwell, pounce pot, wax seal kit. Grey hair in period middle-part with rolled sides and era hairpins. Victorian damask wallpaper with age discoloration. 50mm T1.3, Deakins candle-only.

## CN-004: Terminal Velocity
> High-angle crane shot straight down concrete stairwell spiraling to darkness. 38-year-old man in rumpled suit descending rapidly, face upturned in paranoid check. Vertigo-inspired geometric spiral. Harsh overhead fluorescents at each landing — green-tinged institutional pools. Genuine stress: dilated pupils, hairline perspiration, lip flush. Suit pulled at shoulder, collar riding up. Concrete scuff marks, iron banisters with worn paint. Motion blur at periphery, face sharp. 24mm T2.8.

## CN-005: Harvest Dance
> Summer evening — couple dancing alone in golden wheat field at sunset. Long lens compresses background to wall of grain. Faded blue cotton sundress flares with real weight and drape. Man's white linen rolled to elbows, tanned forearms. Setting sun behind creates pure rim lighting, faces lit only by reflected sky-glow. Summer flush, natural perspiration glow. Individual wheat stalks as golden backlit lines. Dust and chaff float in air. Private unperformed joy. 200mm T2.8 Steadicam.

## CN-006: Neon Confession
> Modern noir motel room. 40-year-old man on bed edge in red-blue glow of exterior neon pulsing through venetian blinds — colored stripes shift with sign rhythm. White undershirt, yellowed collar, chest hair visible. Cigarette smoke volumetric in color bands. Three-day stubble, heavy lids, red-veined eyes. Scene shifts between blue and red. Cheap motel: particle-board, tube TV reflecting room, thin pilled bedspread. Smoke rises vertically, disperses at blinds. 40mm anamorphic T2.8, locked-off. Storaro color narrative.

## CN-007: First Contact
> Vast research facility. 50-year-old female scientist confronts hovering obsidian artifact emitting gravitational distortion visible as light-bending at edges. Low angle, wide lens. Industrial ceiling fixtures harsh overhead while artifact emits otherworldly blue filling shadows. Complex emotion: wide moist eyes, parted lips, micro-tremor of excitement. Lab coat with coffee stain, crooked badge, worn pen clip. Oscilloscopes, cable management, grey racks. Proper gravitational lensing physics. 21mm Zeiss T1.3.

## CN-008: The Morning After Waterloo
> Dawn 1815 battlefield. 35-year-old field surgeon in blood-stained shirt sleeves, period medical kit on ammunition-crate table. Medium dolly tracking. Dawn enters frame-left as cold blue-pink gradually warming. Profound fatigue — sunken purple-undertone eyes, two-day stubble, dried blood in knuckle creases. Hand-stitched cotton shirt with bloodstain darkening. Morning mist rises from damp grass. Guttering candle with natural wax drip patterns. Individual dew drops on grass blades. 40mm Cooke S4 T2, Lubezki natural light.

## CN-009: Pursuit
> 33-year-old woman running through multi-level car park at night. Steadicam follows at her pace — urgent persistent framing. Overhead concrete beams create rhythm. Sodium ceiling pools of sickly orange — enters and exits. Wide sclera-showing eyes, flared nostrils, hard-breathing chin-set. Disheveled hair with realistic momentum. Blouse pulled from waistband, one broken heel causing uneven gait, spine sweat pattern. Adrenaline flush at chest and neck, forearm goosebumps. 27mm Arri T1.8.

## CN-010: Floating World
> 40-year-old Japanese woman in deep indigo silk kimono with white crane embroidery, seated in wooden boat on still dawn lake. Crane descending from high establishing to water-level intimate. Kimono pools naturally over boat edge showing real habutae silk weight. Dawn pastel sky reflects perfectly — boat suspended between two skies. Fine eye lines from expression. Cherry blossoms float — some face-up, some waterlogged. Carved boat with aged lacquer wear. Hair with single lacquered wooden pin. Surface mist. 65mm Panavision Primo T2.

## CN-011: Dead Letter Office
> 1948 noir interior. 42-year-old woman in fitted wool gabardine suit, veiled hat, cigarette holder between satin-gloved fingers. Slightly low angle. Single desk lamp creates classic noir — veil casts precise cage shadow on features. Period silhouette from undergarments. Elbow-length satin gloves with real finger-wrinkle behavior. Smoke rises in lamp beam. Frosted glass door panels with reversed gilt lettering. Rotary Bakelite telephone, manila folders. 50mm Cooke Panchro T2.3, slight dutch tilt.

## CN-012: Terraform
> Partially-terraformed Mars. 45-year-old engineer in dusty worn environmental suit at red-to-green transition zone. Wide anamorphic emphasizing vast horizontal landscape. Sky transitions from red-pink to blue-grey at zenith. Suit shows years of dust in seams, repaired patches, visor polished center with hazed edges. Face through visor with refraction and internal reflections. Fine dust hangs longer in thin atmosphere — reduced-gravity behavior. 40mm Master Anamorphic T1.9.

## CN-013: The Abdication
> Renaissance Italian palace. 60-year-old nobleman removes heavy gold chain of office — shot from behind, rack focusing to mirror reflection showing face. Tall arched window breaks sunlight into individual beams through leaded glass. Chain shows period metalwork with tool marks and age-worn lustre. Burgundy velvet doublet with nap shading. Venetian mercury mirror: slight distortion, grey tone, degraded backing spots. Intarsia woodwork, tapestry with visible weave. 75mm Cooke S7 T2, Storaro Renaissance palette.

## CN-014: Extraction
> Covert document extraction in high-security archive. Shot from inside shelf row looking through folder gaps — voyeuristic frame-within-frame. Cold institutional fluorescent flat lighting. Maintenance coveralls slightly oversized, embroidered name patch that isn't hers, latex gloves. Controlled calm masking tension: widened nostrils, jaw clench, systematic scanning eyes. Greenish skin undertone in fluorescent. Organized boxes, color-coded labels, security camera red blinks. 35mm Zeiss T1.3, static. Pakula tension.

## CN-015: Last Summer
> Late afternoon sun-drenched kitchen. 52-year-old man and 48-year-old woman prepare meal in comfortable silence. Shot from outside through open window — frame creates natural vignette. Long partnership intimacy — passing ingredients without looking. Golden shafts with kitchen steam and particles. Both aging: his receding grey, her laugh lines and pushed-up glasses. Worn chambray, paint-stained apron. Kitchen life: collected pottery, children's artwork on fridge. 50mm Cooke S4 T2.8, static window-frame. Lubezki domestic intimacy.

## CN-016: The Informant

## CN-018: The Coronation Rehearsal

## CN-020: The Lighthouse
> Remote lighthouse interior. 60-year-old woman tends massive Fresnel lens as storm hammers glass. Beam sweeps in rotation casting moving light bars across face. Shot from within lens — prism-split refractions create periodic color washes. Weathered by salt air: deeply lined, sun-damaged, clear far-seeing eyes. Thick fisherman's knit, silver braided hair. Fresnel rings show accurate light-bending. Rain creates water-distortion on exterior glass. Rotating beam as metronome rhythm. 35mm Panavision T2, handheld with storm sway. Deakins isolation.

## CN-021: The Last Witness

Chipped mug, tannin staining. Bakelite ashtray with varied-length crushed butts. Swinging bulb slowly changes shadow geometry. 50mm T2, static.

Alien-colored terrain through window. 50mm Arri T1.8, focus between window surface and interior.

## CN-024: Safe House
> 3 AM safe house bathroom. 30-year-old woman cleans forearm wound using mirror for guidance — shot entirely as mirror reflection, real figure out-of-focus in foreground. Wound shows irregular edges, dermis layers, partially dried margins. Improvised supplies: sewing kit, minibar vodka, torn towels. Perspiration, goosebumps, fading adrenaline flush. Mirror's green tint and peripheral distortion. 40mm T1.8. Bigelow field-medicine.
