## UW-001: Coral Cathedral Light Beams
**Category:** Underwater | **Subcategory:** Coral Reef
**Best Platform:** NanoBanana

> A vast coral bommie rising from a sandy floor at eight meters depth, photographed looking upward with a 15mm rectilinear wide-angle lens as morning sunlight penetrates the surface above in dramatic crepuscular rays. The light beams angle through the blue water column, illuminating suspended plankton particles like floating dust in a cathedral. Hard and soft corals create an archway framing the sun position above, with fan corals in silhouette and table corals catching direct illumination showing polyp detail. Natural blue-cyan water color deepens progressively toward frame edges away from the light source. A school of anthias fish hover in the light beam zone, their orange-pink bodies creating warm accents against the cool water. No artificial color correction has been applied, maintaining the authentic depth-related color absorption.

**Key Techniques:** Crepuscular ray capture through surface, natural blue color shift at depth, silhouette framing against surface light
