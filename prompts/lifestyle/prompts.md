## LS-001: First Light Coffee Ritual
**Category:** Lifestyle | **Subcategory:** Morning Routine
**Best Platform:** NanoBanana

> A woman in her early thirties wrapping both hands around a handmade ceramic mug at a sun-drenched kitchen table at 7:15am, photographed with a 50mm lens at f/1.8 from across the table. She wears an oversized vintage band t-shirt slipping off one shoulder and her hair is genuinely sleep-tousled—not styled to appear messy. The morning light enters from a large east window creating a warm rectangle across the reclaimed wood table surface. Steam rises from the mug in lazy spirals caught by the backlight. Her expression is private, inward—the unguarded face of someone who hasn't yet performed for the world. The kitchen behind shows authentic morning evidence: an open jar of honey with the dipper resting across it, a half-read paperback face-down, toast crumbs on a cutting board.

**Key Techniques:** Backlit steam illumination, unguarded private expression, morning evidence storytelling
