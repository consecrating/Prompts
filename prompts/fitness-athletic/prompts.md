# Fitness & Athletic Prompts

> Professional photographer's art director briefs for fitness and athletic imagery.

## FA-001: Dawn Deadlift Power
**Category:** Fitness & Athletic | **Subcategory:** Gym/Training
**Best Platform:** NanoBanana

> A powerful female athlete mid-deadlift in a raw concrete gym at golden hour, captured from a low three-quarter angle with a 35mm lens at f/2.0. Her back muscles are sharply defined under warm directional light streaming through industrial windows, casting long shadows across the rubberized floor. She wears a charcoal compression sports bra and high-waisted training shorts that stretch authentically over her engaged quadriceps. Fine beads of sweat catch the light along her spine and forearms. The chalk dust suspended in the air creates volumetric light rays. Her skin shows natural pores, micro-veins on her forearms, and the slight flush of exertion across her shoulders. The barbell plates show worn edges and authentic gym patina.

**Key Techniques:** Low-angle power composition, volumetric golden hour light, texture isolation on skin
