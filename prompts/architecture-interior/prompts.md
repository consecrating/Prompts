## AI-001: Cantilever Pavilion Dawn
**Category:** Architecture & Interior | **Subcategory:** Modern Architecture
**Best Platform:** NanoBanana

> A dramatically cantilevered concrete pavilion extending over a cliff edge at dawn, photographed from below with a 16mm ultra-wide lens at f/11. The structure defies visual gravity—its mass appears to float over the void with only the recessed support visible deep within the building. The board-formed concrete shows every plank impression, tie-hole pattern, and the natural aggregate exposed at weathered edges. Morning light rakes across the underside revealing construction joints and the patina of several seasons. Native grasses at the cliff edge provide organic contrast to geometric precision. A single figure stands at the glass railing providing human scale reference against the vast landscape below—rolling hills dissolving into morning mist layers.

**Key Techniques:** Ultra-wide cantilever drama, board-formed concrete texture, human scale reference
