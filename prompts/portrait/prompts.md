# Portrait Photography Prompts (30)

## P-001: The Architect's Contemplation
> A 58-year-old South Asian male architect standing beside a large drafting table in his sunlit studio, wearing a rumpled linen shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows. Rembrandt lighting from a north-facing industrial window casts a triangular highlight on his left cheek. Wire-rimmed glasses catch a subtle reflection. Skin shows natural age spots, visible pores across the nose bridge, fine creases from decades of focus. Background: rolled blueprints and models in soft focus. 85mm f/1.8, warm amber highlights, cool blue-grey shadows. Graphite dust on fingertips.

## P-002: Power in Stillness
> A 34-year-old Black female executive against deep charcoal seamless. Structured navy wool blazer with visible weave over cream silk blouse. Butterfly lighting from large octabox above creates defined cheekbone shadows. Natural 4C hair in sculptural updo. Skin shows luminosity with subtle hyperpigmentation, visible pores, tiny beauty mark near left eyebrow. Blazer compression wrinkles at elbow. 110mm f/4 medium format, thin rim separation from hairlight.

## P-003: The Woodworker's Hands
> A 62-year-old Caucasian craftsman in his woodworking shop, slightly low angle. Worn canvas apron with sawdust in creases, faded flannel with frayed collar. Split lighting from a hanging shop lamp — one half harsh shadows, other half warm amber from reflected wood. Hands on half-finished walnut table showing veins, healed nicks, calloused fingertips. Wood shavings catch backlight. Warm golden particulates floating in the beam. 50mm f/2.8.

## P-004: Morning Light Meditation

Dark hair with flyaways catching backlight as golden strands. Tight headshot, negative space above. 135mm f/2, peach and sage tones.
